## Key Design Decisions

- `CliError::Display` sanitizes internal details (CWE-209) - generic messages for `Internal`, `ServerError`, `NotFound`
- `NotImplemented` variant carries the user-facing message for stub handlers
- `SiemClient` validates headers for CR/LF/null injection (CWE-113)
- `ClientConfig::with_overrides()` enables safe testing without `set_var`
- `OutputFormatter` delegates to comfy-table (table), serde_json (JSON), csv crate (CSV)
