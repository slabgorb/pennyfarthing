## Delivery Findings

- **Gap** (blocking): `ApiResponse<T>` in `crates/axiathon-core/src/types.rs` does not match AC specification. Current type has `error: Option<ApiError>` (singular) but AC requires `errors: Vec<ApiError>` (always-present array). Current `ApiMeta` has pagination fields but AC requires `request_id`, `timestamp`, `trace_id`. Affects `crates/axiathon-core/src/types.rs` (needs restructuring).
  *Found by TEA during test design.*

- **Gap** (non-blocking): `ApiResponse` uses `skip_serializing_if = "Option::is_none"` on all fields, but the AC requires `errors` and `meta` to always be present in the serialized JSON (even when empty/default). Affects `crates/axiathon-core/src/types.rs` (remove skip_serializing_if on errors and meta).
  *Found by TEA during test design.*
