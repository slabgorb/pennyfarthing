# Theme Personality Comparison Guide

How to choose a theme based on personality dimensions and team fit.

> **102 themes available** - All themes include OCEAN (Big Five) personality profiles, Chernoff face visualizations, and spider charts. This guide covers personality theory and highlights selected themes. Run `/list-themes` to see all available themes.

## Personality Dimensions

## Big Five Personality Traits (OCEAN Model)

The Big Five personality model provides a scientific framework for understanding theme differences. Each trait represents a spectrum from low to high.

### The Five Traits

| Trait | Low Score | High Score |
|-------|-----------|------------|
| **O**penness | Practical, conventional, routine-focused | Creative, curious, adventurous |
| **C**onscientiousness | Flexible, spontaneous, improvisational | Organized, disciplined, goal-oriented |
| **E**xtraversion | Reserved, solitary, reflective | Outgoing, energetic, expressive |
| **A**greeableness | Competitive, skeptical, challenging | Cooperative, trusting, supportive |
| **N**euroticism | Calm, stable, emotionally resilient | Anxious, reactive, emotionally intense |

## Theme Profiles

### Dark/Cynical Themes

| Theme | Style | Best For |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **The Expanse** | Gritty realism, noir undertones, competent pragmatism | Teams who prefer realistic stakes, no sugarcoating |
| **Legion of Doom** | Villainous camp, theatrical scheming, bombastic | Teams who enjoy ironic humor, villain energy |
| **Discworld** | Dark British humor, philosophical depth, wit | Teams who appreciate satire and Pratchett's worldview |

### Light/Optimistic Themes

| Theme | Style | Best For |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Ted Lasso** | Relentless positivity, folksy wisdom, growth mindset | Teams focused on culture and psychological safety |
| **Superfriends** | Heroic declarations, campy optimism, 80s energy | Teams who want upbeat, uncomplicated heroics |
| **Parks & Rec** | Earnest enthusiasm, workplace comedy, binders | Teams who appreciate bureaucratic humor, Leslie Knope energy |
| **Star Trek TNG** | Idealistic professionalism, diplomatic, principled | Teams who value process and collaboration |

### Theatrical/Dramatic Themes

| Theme | Style | Best For |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Shakespeare** | Iambic pentameter, soliloquies, classical drama | Teams who appreciate literary flourish and quotability |
| **Star Trek TOS** | Bold leadership, dramatic pauses, Kirk energy | Teams who want decisive, action-oriented personas |
| **Legion of Doom** | Over-the-top villain speeches, campy declarations | Teams embracing dramatic irony |
| **Superfriends** | Heroic exclamations, "Great Scott!" energy | Teams wanting unironic enthusiasm |

### Understated/Subtle Themes

| Theme | Style | Best For |
|-------|-------|----------|
| **Jane Austen** | Quiet devastation, social wit, raised eyebrows | Teams appreciating sophisticated, understated humor |
| **The Expanse** | Terse competence, actions over words | Teams preferring minimal personality overhead |
| **A-Team** | Cocky confidence, 80s action banter | Teams wanting action-oriented but practical |

## Most Disparate Pairs

These theme pairs sit at maximum personality distance:

| Theme A | Theme B | The Contrast |
|---------|---------|--------------|
| **Jane Austen** | **Legion of Doom** | Quiet social wit vs bombastic villainy. Elizabeth Bennet's raised eyebrow vs Sinestro's yellow power ring of judgment. |
| **Ted Lasso** | **The Expanse** | "Believe!" vs "Doors and corners." Relentless optimism vs noir fatalism. Biscuits vs bullets. |
| **Shakespeare** | **A-Team** | Iambic pentameter vs "I ain't committin' no untested code, fool!" Classical theater vs 80s machismo. |
| **Discworld** | **Superfriends** | DEATH speaking IN CAPITALS about curry vs "GREAT SCOTT!" heroics. Dark philosophy vs bright optimism. |

### Why Disparity Matters

Disparate themes reveal personality preferences:
- If you love Jane Austen and hate Legion of Doom → You prefer subtle wit over theatrical camp
- If you love The Expanse and hate Ted Lasso → You prefer realism over optimism
- The theme you gravitate toward reveals your team's communication style

## Changing Themes

To switch themes:

```yaml
# .pennyfarthing/config.local.yaml
theme: the-expanse    # Change this value
```

Or use the CLI: `pennyfarthing theme set the-expanse`

All agents adopt new characters immediately. No restart required.
