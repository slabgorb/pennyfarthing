### Data Source
Benchmark data lives in `results/benchmarks/` with this structure:
```

└── {scenario}/
    └── {theme}-{role}/

        │   ├── judge_1.json
        │   └── ...
        └── summary.yaml
```

### Summary.yaml Format
```yaml
agent:
  theme: discworld
  role: dev
  spec: discworld:dev

scenario:
  name: race-condition-cache

statistics:
  n: 4
  mean: 88.50
  std_dev: 1.5
  min: 87
  max: 90
  scores: [87, 90, 90, 87]

baseline_comparison:
  control_mean: 76.80
  control_stddev: 0.63
  delta: +11.70
```
